# Tuning run: 2026-10-17, with CBD Indoor Variants

Issue [#7](https://github.com/blurboy1985/haze-gowhere/issues/7), [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-osm-indoor-last-mile-in-cbd.md). Same harness and weights as [2026-10-16](2026-10-16.md), plus three CBD trips. Candidates are planned for 12:00 SGT (inside Indoor Link hours) and include Indoor Variants built from `server/data/cbd-indoor-graph.json`.

## Findings

1. **CBD station-to-mall walks lose most of their Exposed time.** Bugis Junction → Suntec goes from 6 Exposed min (Bus 133) to 1 at Band 2 (Bus 133 + indoor link, +1 min) and 0 at Band 3+ (EWL + Esplanade Xchange/Suntec, 14 min). Raffles Place MRT → Suntec: 0 Exposed (NSL + indoor, 14 min). Tanjong Pagar → Marina Square: 1 Exposed (EWL + indoor).
2. **Nothing changes outside the CBD.** None of the 15 non-CBD trips gets an Indoor Variant. Their picks differ slightly from 2026-10-16 only because OneMap's timetable differs at 12:00.
3. **Raffles Place → Marina Bay Sands is unchanged at 6 Exposed min.** The MBS hotel end lands on an indoor fragment that the Shoppes connector doesn't reach (see `scripts/fixtures/indoor-connectors.json`). It needs an OSM survey, not a guess.
4. **Band 1 still picks the faster street walk where one exists** (Bugis → Suntec, Bus 133, 10 min), as intended by showing the indoor version as an extra variant.
5. The weights need no change: Indoor Variants win on Exposure, not on tuning.
