Issue [#5](https://github.com/blurboy1985/haze-gowhere/issues/5). Generated by `npx tsx scripts/tune-trips.ts --refresh` over the 16 Trips in `scripts/fixtures/tuning-trips.json`. The data includes real OneMap routes, all three mode queries (#4), and LTA linkways (#3).

## Findings

1. **Bands 2, 3 and 4 always pick the same Route.** K=1 at Band 2 already switches to the less-Exposed option whenever one exists. K=3 and K=6 never changed a pick on this sample. With OneMap's small candidate sets, the bands are effectively binary: Band 1 picks the fastest Route, and Band 2+ picks the least Exposed.
2. **Every switch looked right.** Each was a large cut in Exposed time for at most 3 extra minutes. The biggest: Changi T3 → Tampines Hub, from MRT (32 min, 19 min Exposed) to Bus 27 → 20 (33 min, 4 min Exposed).
3. **The 2× slowdown cap never removed a meaningfully less-Exposed Route** (flag 1 never fired).
4. **The limit is candidate variety, not the weights.** Bugis → Suntec (Bus 133, 6 min Exposed) and Raffles Place → Marina Bay Sands (6 min Exposed) ignore the enclosed underground links (CityLink Mall, Marina Bay Link Mall) that a local would use. This is the gap ADR 0001 / #7 describes.
5. **Jurong East → NUS UTown** still has 10 Exposed minutes at Band 4. At that level the Taxi Suggestion is the right advice, and it is shown from Band 3.

## Proposal (for decision)

- **Keep K = 0/1/3/6, the ¼ Covered weight, and the 2× cap.** Nothing in this sample argues for a change.
- Optionally soften Band 2 to K=0.5, so that Band 2 only switches when the saving is at least 2 Exposed min per extra minute. No trip in this sample would change.
- Re-run this harness whenever the candidate source changes (for example a new OneMap mode, or indoor links if #7 triggers).
